## 2. Full Store Description (en-US)
*(Max 4,000 characters)*

```text
JobPrep is your all-in-one personal dashboard for tracking job circulars, staying ahead of application deadlines, and systematically preparing for competitive exams (BCS, Bank, Government, and Private careers).

Designed with a subtle, distraction-free glassmorphic interface, JobPrep puts your daily priorities first: know exactly what application deadline is closing next, what topics to study today, and how close you are to your goals.

KEY FEATURES:

📌 CIRCULAR TRACKING & LIVE DEADLINES
• Organize application deadlines, admit card releases, and exam dates in one place.
• Live color-coded countdowns warn you 7, 3, and 1 days before deadlines close.
• Attach circular PDFs, admit cards, and application forms securely.

📥 SHARE TO QUICK CAPTURE
• Found an important job circular or exam announcement on Facebook, LinkedIn, or the web?
• Tap Android's Share button and select JobPrep to prefill the link and save it directly to your Inbox in a single tap.

🔒 ZERO-KNOWLEDGE ENCRYPTED CREDENTIAL VAULT
• Never lose your applicant login credentials again.
• Store portal user IDs and passwords protected by client-side AES-GCM encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation.
• Your passphrase is never sent to our servers, logged, or stored unencrypted.

📚 PRECISION STUDY PLANNER & WEIGHTED PROGRESS
• Break down exam syllabi into subjects and granular materials (e.g., books with exact page counts).
• True weighted progress: completing a 400-page book in a 1,000-page syllabus accurately reflects 40% of your subject goal.
• Set study paces and rest days with automatic catch-up scheduling if you miss a day.

⏱️ FOCUS STUDY TIMER & POMODORO
• Customizable focus and break timers with ambient progress rings.
• Automatically logs minutes studied and units completed to your dashboard.

🔁 SPACED REPETITION REVISION QUEUE
• Mark difficult concepts and chapters for revision.
• Systematic spaced intervals ensure you review high-yield topics before exam day.

🔔 SMART NOTIFICATIONS & WEEKLY REVIEWS
• In-app notifications and optional email digests for closing deadlines and upcoming exams.
• Automated weekly review screens celebrate study streaks and identify subjects needing attention.

🌐 FULL BILINGUAL SUPPORT & ACCESSIBILITY
• Native English and Bangla UI with Noto Sans Bengali typography and optional Bengali numerals.
• Dark Mode and Light Mode designed with high-contrast accessibility tokens.
• "Reduce Effects" toggle for smooth performance on any device.

📦 PRIVACY & DATA OWNERSHIP
• Zero ads, zero trackers, and zero third-party data broker sharing.
• Export all circulars and study logs to CSV or JSON anytime.
• Complete, irreversible account deletion available right inside Settings.

Start preparing with clarity and focus. Download JobPrep today!
```
